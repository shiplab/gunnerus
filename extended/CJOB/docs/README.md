## Weight calculation

Light-ship weight breakdown with CG coordinates (LCG/TCG/VCG) and moments (Mx/My/Mz) per item — hull, cargo equipment, ship equipment, crew/passenger equipment and more — totalling **411.79 t** light ship weight. The `.xlsx` is the live calculation; the `.pdf` is the plotted issue.

## Gross/net tonnage calculation

IMO International Convention on Tonnage Measurement of Ships 1969 (Annex I) calculation: total moulded volume, cargo-space volume, passenger/berth counts and the resulting gross and net tonnage figures. The `.xlsm` is the live calculation; the `.pdf` is the plotted issue.

## Specification

A 12-page preliminary vessel specification: general information and definitions, units of measurement, environmental characteristics, vessel type, navigation areas and operating conditions, main dimensions, capacities, general arrangement and compartment layout, speed and stability, and further sections beyond what the table of contents on page 3 previews.

## List of project documents

The authoritative index of C-JOB's `25.1142` project deliverables — 19 entries, each with its document number, name, revision and sheet format. It is the source for the document numbers cited against each drawing in [`../README.md`](../README.md). Two entries on this list are **not included in this repository**: `25.1142-000-020 Freeboard Calculation` and a `25.1142-100-123 Shell Expansion` deliverable (a Shell Expansion drawing does exist in the repository, but only the polarkonsult original — see [`../../../polarkonsult/README.md`](../../../polarkonsult/README.md)).

## List of main equipment

A 9-page numbered equipment list grouped by system, starting with lifesaving equipment (MOB boat, rescue boat davit, life rafts, survival suits, work vests, life jackets, lifebuoys, ...) and continuing through the vessel's other main systems.
