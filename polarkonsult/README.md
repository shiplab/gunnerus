## General arrangement

`GA Gunnerus Rev1`: profile and inboard profile, A-deck, 1-deck, below-1-deck and tank-top plans, wheelhouse, a section at frame 17 and the view from aft, with the main dimensions and capacity tables reproduced on sheet. This is the drawing [`../metadata/gunnerus_metadata.json`](../metadata/README.md) is extracted from.

## Tank plan

`Tank Plan Rev1`: tank arrangement in profile and on the tank top, with a tank schedule giving frame range, net volume and mass per fluid type for tanks 1–15 and the cargo hold aft — **202.42 m³** net volume in total, tanks 14/15 (added in the lengthening) folded into the same table as the rest.

## Lines plan

`Lines Plan Rev0`: the moulded hull form as sheer plan, body plan and half-breadth plan, with the vessel's main particulars table — the source for the particulars quoted in the main [README](../README.md#vessel).

## Profile and plan

`Profile and Plan Rev0`: hull structure — section at the centreline and at 2700–1400 FCL starboard, the 1-deck plan, and the tanktops and single bottom — with scantlings, girder and pillar arrangements and welding details. Sheet 1 of 2.

## Midship section

`Midship Section Rev0`: transverse structural sections through the midbody (frames 22, 23, 32, sections A–I and bulkhead 200 A.F. #1), with scantlings, new and existing bulkheads, the old and new baselines from the vessel's lengthening, welding details and a keel section.

## Shell expansion

`Shell Expansion Rev0`: shell plating developed over the ship's length, with plate thicknesses, seams and butts, tank boundaries, sea chest, HiPAP opening and knuckle lines. Two sheets — **1/2 starboard side, 2/2 port side** — despite the single file name.
