How the links are made
  • 3D model: each part in derived/3d/gunnerus-parts.json names its SFI group and, where matched, the item number in the C-JOB list of main equipment. Two matches are tentative (anchor, workboat).
  • Equipment list and specification: both PDFs are parsed as text. A specification line is linked when it names the item.
  • Drawings: the labels in each drawing's DXF file are searched with a keyword pattern per item; the matching label is shown as evidence. A drawing can show an item without labelling it, so a missing link does not mean the item is absent. The lines plan is left out of the search because its DXF also holds a copy of the GA labels.
  • GA metadata: equipment entries are linked by hand, only where the match is clear.
  • Weight calculation: the eight SFI main groups of the C-JOB light-ship weight calculation, with their centres of gravity.
  • Measured data: channels of the wave-shielding case linked to parts by name, averaged over 10 s for the sparklines. The experiment and its set-up are described in Wang et al. (2025), Ocean Engineering 320, 120189, doi:10.1016/j.oceaneng.2024.120189.
  • All links are built by pages/tools/build_explorer_data.py. Found a wrong link? Report it.
