One quantity, many names

RV Gunnerus has 53 signals we followed across the engine vendor, the power management system, HEMOS, the 2023 logger and the 2019 asset model. The same quantity is named differently in each, units and scaling live in separate documents, and some documents disagree with the logged data. This page shows what is logged, what each signal means, where the conventions clash, and what happens when an AI model is asked to translate the names.

What is behind this page

  • metadata/signals/gunnerus-signal-crosswalk.csv: the 53-signal crosswalk (names in each source, quantity, unit, SFI code, status).
  • metadata/signals/translator-test: the blind inputs, the answers of both arms, the key and the grading script.
  • metadata/signals/data: the two 2019 extracts (engine log 1-minute, docking log 10-second).
  • pages/assets/signals/signals.json: what this page reads, built by metadata/signals/scripts/build_page_data.py.

Only our own crosswalk is published here: names, meaning in our words, units and status. Vendor documents and PLC dumps are not republished.
