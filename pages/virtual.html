    About this replay

    What you see. Logged values from the cruise week of 30 October to 3 November 2023, replayed as if live. The page opens at a random moment while the ship is under way. Heading, motion, wind, azimuth angles, crane and engine readings are the logger values at 1 Hz, loaded one day at a time. Operating state, the timeline and the fuel totals use 1-minute means. Wave radar values exist only for the wave-shielding test on 31 October, 10:00 to 12:10 UTC.

    Measured, derived, display only. Values are as logged. Course in the 1-minute data, operating state, the event log, which gensets and consumers are connected, and fuel totals are derived. The ship symbol and needles move smoothly between logged values; that smoothing is display only.

    Credit. Data provided by Robert Skulstad (NTNU); organised by Henrique M. Gaspar. The wave-shielding test is the experiment of Wang et al. (2025), Ocean Engineering 320, 120189. Chart © Kartverket.
