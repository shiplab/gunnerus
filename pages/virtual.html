    About this replay

    What you see. Logged values from the cruise week of 30 October to 3 November 2023, replayed as if live. The page opens at a random moment while the ship is under way, or at the moment in the link (for example virtual.html#t=2023-11-03T12:48:02Z, which then plays at 1×). Heading, motion, wind, azimuth angles, crane and engine readings are the logger values at 1 Hz, loaded one day at a time. Operating state, the event log, the fuel totals and the 3-hour trail come from the same 1 Hz values; the timeline of the week uses 1-minute means. Wave radar values exist only for the wave-shielding test on 31 October, 10:00 to 12:10 UTC.

    Measured, derived, display only. Values are as logged. Course in the 1-minute data, operating state (60-second means, same rules as the power and energy case), the event log, which gensets and consumers are connected, and fuel totals are derived. The ship symbol and needles move smoothly between logged values; that smoothing is display only. Ship outlines and equipment positions come from the C-JOB 3D model; roll and pitch drawings are exaggerated.

    Credit. Data provided by Robert Skulstad (NTNU); organised by Henrique M. Gaspar. The wave-shielding test is the experiment of Wang et al. (2025), Ocean Engineering 320, 120189. Chart © Kartverket.
