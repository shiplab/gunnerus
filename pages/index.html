Why this exists

Complete ship data is usually locked away

Design and engineering material for a vessel is normally proprietary. That makes it hard for students and researchers to work on a realistic, complete ship. This repository opens up one real case, so everyone can work on the same vessel.

  • Research

    Ship design, hydrodynamics, structures and digital ship models, on a hull that exists and sails.

  • Teaching

    Student projects and courses built around full drawings, not simplified textbook examples.

  • Benchmarking

    A shared reference for validation and reproducible experiments across groups.

  • Digital tools

    Test data for digital shipbuilding tools, CAD pipelines and exchange formats.

Production drawings · A0 sheets

Fifteen drawings, each in DWG, DXF and PDF

From the general arrangement and lines plan to shell expansion and the fire zone plan. Drawing units are millimetres. Frames run from 0 at the aft perpendicular at 500 mm spacing.

PDF opens GitHub's viewer. DWG (AutoCAD 2018, AC1032) is the authoritative source; DXF is the ASCII exchange format.

metadata/gunnerus_metadata.json

The general arrangement, as data

The GA drawing read into structured JSON: decks and spaces, tanks and equipment. Every entry carries its SFI group code and, where the mapping is unambiguous, a DNV VIS/GMOD v3.10a top-level node, so it joins against other SFI- or VIS-coded datasets.
